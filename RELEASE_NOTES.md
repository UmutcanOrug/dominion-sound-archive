Dominion Sound Archive 1.0.6 for Foundry VTT v13 and SWADE.

Automatic Sound Matching now uses the Sound Archive asset workflow. In Configure Settings, add a character sound set, enter the Actor name, then choose Add Audio File or Add Folder under Attack, Hurt or Death. The picker opens Forge Assets on Forge. Play any file from its own row. Refresh linked folders and save to apply your edits.

Fixes the controls getting stuck after Add sound set. Existing 1.0.5 sound paths load as individual clips.
